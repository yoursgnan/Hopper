Architecture & Features

Designed for uninterrupted workflow.

Hopper uses Chrome's native Document Picture-in-Picture standard to float interactive pages, not just static video tags.

🪟

True Always-on-Top

Floats above full-screen IDEs, office suites, spreadsheets, and games. Stays visible wherever you work.

🫧

170x48 Capsule Mode

Collapse any floating window into an ultra-narrow translucent pill to reclaim 95% of your screen while audio continues.

🎯

Smart Video Focus

Detects active video streams and strips away comments, sidebars, and banners for a distraction-free view.

📱

Mobile Companion

Pop out pages in a smartphone-proportioned 420x760 layout with custom zoom levels for responsive reading.

⚡

1-Click Toolbar Trigger

Click the pinned Hopper mascot icon in your Chrome toolbar to instantly float or dock any active tab.

🔒

100% Client-Side & Private

No background analytics, no telemetry, and zero network calls. Runs entirely on your local machine.

Installation

How to load unpacked in Chrome.

You can install Hopper directly in 4 simple steps without waiting for Chrome Web Store distribution.

1

Download and extract the ZIP

Download the extension files and extract the Hopper folder to your computer.

2

Open Chrome's Extensions page

Open a new tab and paste this URL into your address bar:

chrome://extensions
3

Turn on Developer mode

In the top right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the switch to ON.

Developer mode ● ON
4

Click "Load unpacked"

Click the blue button in the top left, select the extracted Hopper folder, and pin the mascot icon to your toolbar.

Load unpacked → Select extracted Hopper folder
